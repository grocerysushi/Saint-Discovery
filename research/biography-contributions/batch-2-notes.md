# Batch 2 biography contributions

Reviewed on 2026-09-26. Scope: the 115 existing slug keys in `lib/data/saint-biography-expansions-2.json`, from David I of Scotland through John Francis Regis.

## Method and result

Read the existing expansion biographies and the review-merging logic in `lib/saint-reviews.ts` before drafting. Retrieved the cited sources through browsing, using both full-page retrieval and source text returned in search results. Wrote each addition separately around a concrete new topic and supplied an original, topic-specific study activity. Saved incremental batches throughout the work and maintained the dashboard progress file.

The resulting JSON contains 115 entries, 230 distinct paragraphs, 115 distinct reflection activities, and 116 distinct cited URLs. Paragraph bodies range from 85 to 119 words per entry. Each entry is dated 2026-09-26. No biography, identity, calendar, app, authentication, database, or deployment files were changed by this batch.

The prose distinguishes contemporary evidence, later devotional tradition, textual interpretation, and institutional reception. Orthodox sources remain explicitly Orthodox where relevant; Blessed titles are preserved; Guardian Angels is treated as an observance and Holy Innocents as a collective commemoration. Activities are original suggestions, not quotations or attributed teachings. No direct source quotations were inserted.

## Source limitations and decisions

- USCCB Luke 23 was inaccessible through the browsing tool during Dismas research. The addition uses the retrieved Nominis account and does not invent a pre-crucifixion life.
- The Vatican City State Florian and Frances of Rome pages repeatedly failed retrieval. Florian uses the Diocese of Linz account of later patronage; Frances uses the retrieved Catholic Encyclopedia article.
- The original Barbarigo Vatican City State page failed retrieval. The Padua seminary library supplies a much more specific account of the press, its teaching purpose, type, and publications. Its 1684 date was preferred to a conflicting older diocesan short article.
- The original Jacinta Vatican City State page failed retrieval. The Shrine of Fatima's memorial report supplies burial-translation history; old uses of Blessed in that archival report do not determine her present title.
- The original Jadwiga Vatican homily PDF returned effectively empty content. The equivalent official HTML was retrieved and used. The similarly named Nominis result links to Hedwig traditions, so the addition deliberately uses the canonization homily for Queen Jadwiga.
- Euphrasia's OCA January 12 source concerns the mother, Eupraxia the Elder. The contribution explicitly treats that account as family context for the daughter, not as a second biography of the same person.
- Eulalia's Catholic Encyclopedia page contains traditions of both Barcelona and Merida. The addition uses its expressly Merida institutional history and avoids transferring Barcelona's martyr narrative.
- Germaine's Nominis page contains incompatible household descriptions in its main text and an appended correction. The addition makes the discrepancy visible without claiming to settle it.
- Some short source pages were supplemented by official sources: the Directory on Popular Piety for Guardian Angels, a primary Ignatius letter for his companions, the Jesuit explanation of the Spiritual Exercises, the 2022 Vatican decree for Irenaeus, and university history for Regis.
- A Carmelite CITOC page for Bakanja failed with HTTP 406. The final addition uses the retrieved Czech-Moravian Carmelite delegation account. The latter's source passage was available in the browsing search response.
- Nominis John Francis Regis failed retrieval. The final legacy addition uses the university's own 2021 historical article and catalog instead.
- The www form of the English Eudist source failed, but the cited non-www URL retrieved successfully.
- The dated Catholic Encyclopedia remains useful for textual history and historical reception, but its polemical language, old feast dates, and unqualified miracle claims were not adopted. Its personal-life claims were qualified where the baseline review already marked uncertainty.
- The Cantius parish source contains an apparent canonization-date typo, and the Regis catalog calls its seventeenth-century patron eighteenth-century. Neither error was used in the additions.
- No unresolved retrieval issue prevented completion: inaccessible sources were replaced, or the addition was confined to evidence actually retrieved.
