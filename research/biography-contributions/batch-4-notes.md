# Batch 4 source and editorial notes

115 individually written additions; existing biographies and researched identities preserved. Reviewed September 26, 2026. Additions distinguish narrative traditions, devotional interpretation, and documented institutions. Reflections are original study activities, not saint quotations. These are AI-assisted source comparisons, not a claim of expert theological review.

Initial retrievals and page text are retained in source-cache-4. Some OCA pages failed shell fetching but were retrieved through the web tool. Additional sources were retrieved and read through the web tool, including Patrick’s letter, papal audiences and homilies, the Servite Peregrine page, and the full Rita and Rafqa biographies. Only cited sources actually read were used. Dates and questionable details in older sources were omitted where unsupported or inconsistent. Modern Scripture translations were not copied.

Particular cautions: Ursula, Veronica, Valentine and other early lives separate cult evidence from later story. Vladimir is situated in the peoples of Rus’ without equating medieval boundaries with a modern state. Solanus remains Blessed. Marian observances and Raphael are not presented as ordinary human biographies. The Walburga entry avoids the outdated attribution to her of works by Hugeburc. Willibrord avoids the older encyclopedia’s erroneous papal number. The Wolfgang entry omits that source’s mistaken identification of Prague’s first bishop.
