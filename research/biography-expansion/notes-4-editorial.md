# Batch 4 editorial follow-up

Reviewed September 16, 2026. Changes limited to the eleven profiles identified during the independent editorial read. No root long-profile entries changed.

## Changes

- Peter Damian: chronological progression from formation through papal appointments to death and later recognition; removed duplicated Doctor-of-the-Church and mission descriptions. Used the existing Vatican News page; did not import its chronologically problematic sentence about working with Gregory VII as pope.
- Severinus: replaced repeated comments about Eugippius with concrete food/clothing distribution, prisoner-release negotiations, and evacuation assistance supported by the existing Vatican City State page.
- Paul the Simple: replaced repeated generic formation descriptions with the traditional three-day wait, separate cell, particular deliverance narrative, and calendar observances; miracle testimony remains explicitly qualified.
- Pachomius: removed the duplicated angelic-rule qualification; added Pabau, differentiated ascetic arrangements, and meal provision from the existing encyclopedia article.
- Scholastica: kept the storm narrative together; separated the subsequent dove/burial tradition and historical limits.
- William of Eskhill: replaced generic descriptions of reform with the return of his French companions, relocation to Æbelholt circa 1175, teaching subjects, almsgiving, financial pressures, pilgrimage, and local commemoration. Added the official Danish Catholic Church article. The old encyclopedia says death1202; Danish Church says1203. The biography explicitly identifies both rather than silently contradicting existing metadata. The Danish June16 feast is distinguished from April6 in older calendars.
- Richard of Chichester: removed the editorial comparison with a former incorrect catalog date; retained April3.
- Wenceslaus: clarified pilgrimage at Stará Boleslav and the specific2009 veneration of relics; did not imply his principal tomb is there.
- Vincent Ferrer, Vladimir, Yves: trimmed repetitions to bring each single-source profile below200 words.
