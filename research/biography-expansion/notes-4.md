# Biography expansion batch 4

Reviewed September 16, 2026. Coverage: all 115 published entries from Nicholas Owen through Zita. AI-assisted research and editing, not ecclesiastical approval or scholarly peer review.

## Research and scope

Existing source references were starting points for individual consultation. The expanded prose adds circumstances of vocation, relationships, institutional work, writings, reception, and relevant historical limitations. Four paragraphs are the baseline. Eight profiles receive six-paragraph treatments using distinct additional sources: Patrick, Peter, Paul, Padre Pio, Teresa of Ávila, Thérèse of Lisieux, Thomas Aquinas, and Vincent de Paul. The first paragraph stays short enough to function as a card summary.

The source trail for each entry is stored alongside its biography in `lib/data/saint-biography-expansions-4.json`. References include Holy See biographies, catecheses and homilies; Vatican News; official Jesuit, Carmelite, shrine and diocesan material; the French bishops' Nominis calendar; Orthodox Church in America; and the historical Catholic Encyclopedia. Patrick's own Confession is consulted through the Royal Irish Academy edition. Scripture references use the USCCB Bible.

The older Encyclopedia is useful for source criticism and the history of veneration but can contain outdated chronology and historical assumptions. Institutional provenance does not make every detail on a source page reliable. Miraculous episodes, late martyr narratives, private revelations, and uncertain journeys are attributed to their traditions. Biblical people and Marian observances are not given invented modern biographies. No new quotations or patronage claims were added.

## Access and factual qualifications

- An unavailable Nominis response for Severinus was supplemented by its indexed text and the Vatican City State account identifying Eugippius's work and date.
- Stanley Rother's earlier article was inaccessible; the Archdiocese of Oklahoma City's dedicated biography supplied a working replacement.
- Zélie Martin's old canonization PDF was inaccessible; the Alençon sanctuary's individual biography supplied her business and family history. It is distinct from the source used for Louis Martin.
- Stanislaus Kostka's general article about novices was too thin; the Society of Jesus's dedicated biography supplied the Vienna, Germany, and Rome narrative.
- Vincent of Saragossa and Zita had very short calendar notices; separate Encyclopedia articles supplied substantive historical context.
- Sources sometimes contain evident errors: the Guadalupe article gives a problematic Juan Diego canonization date, Raphael's article mixes biblical chronology, and the Rafqa account has inconsistent numerals. Those details were not imported. The older Willibrord article's papal numbering was not repeated.
- The rich narrative for Vladimir is treated as Orthodox reception, without importing its modern territorial or national claims. Kyivan Rus' is distinguished from modern nation-states.
- Tekle Haymanot, Paul the Simple, Theodore of Sykeon, and other ascetic narratives are presented as hagiography; extreme practices are not prescriptions for readers.
- The martyrdom account for Perfectus is not generalized into collective responsibility for Muslims. Rita's difficult marriage and Zita's mistreatment are not presented as obligations to endure abuse.

The Pedro Calungsod expansion uses the 2012 canonization homily; other batch editors used separate individual sources for saints canonized on that occasion. Timothy and Titus share the short 2006 audience background, with distinct biblical chapters supplying their new paragraphs. The eight longer profiles use separate second sources so one page is not stretched into a long derivative biography.
