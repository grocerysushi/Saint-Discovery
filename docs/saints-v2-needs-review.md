# Saints v2: needs review

Saints at templateVersion 1.5 are listed here: a required v2 field was left empty rather than guessed, so their pages keep the old layout until resolved.

**2026-11-11:** the site owner made *symbols* optional for v2. All 63 saints previously held at 1.5 had every other required field, so they moved to v2 with "Symbols in art" hidden; any partial or unconfirmed symbols were removed. Each entry's `flags` in `lib/data/saint-devotions.json` keeps the research notes (patronages without live topics, source conflicts, biography discrepancies).

- `jose-sanchez-del-rio`: held at 1.5 while the existing November 20 fact card is reviewed alongside the November 20 local group memorial and the February 10 individual feast identified by the USCCB and Notre Dame. [Dicastery for the Causes of Saints](https://www.causesanti.va/it/santi-e-beati/jose-sanchez-del-rio.html); [Notre Dame Basilica](https://basilica.nd.edu/prayer-and-worship/global-saints-initiative/st-jose-luis-sanchez-del-rio/); [USCCB](https://www.usccb.org/resources/Jubilee2025_Teenagers.pdf).

- `leonard-of-noblac`: held at 1.5 because no documented historical relationship with another published saint was established. His medieval devotion postdates the uncertain traditional account of his life. [Catholic Encyclopedia](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09178b.htm).
- `longinus`: held at 1.5 pending review of the existing March 15 fact card against other October 16 observances and the uncertain identification of the named figure. [Catholic Church in France](https://nominis.cef.fr/contenus/saint/8577/Saint-Longin.html).

- `lydia`: held at 1.5 pending reconciliation of August 3 and May 20 observances and conflicting statements about the Roman Martyrology. [Catholic Church in France](https://nominis.cef.fr/contenus/saints/1614/Sainte-Lydie.html); [Vatican News](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/saints/08/03.html).
- `magdalena-of-nagasaki`: held at 1.5 pending verification of the existing October 15 fact card alongside the Augustinian October 20 observance and the group memorial on September 28. [Order of St. Augustine](https://augustinian.org/october-20/).
- `marcellinus`: held at 1.5 pending authoritative review of current recognition and the historical April 26 observance; later accounts of his conduct and death are disputed. [Catholic Encyclopedia](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09637d.htm).

- `maria-guadalupe-garcia-zavala`: held at 1.5 pending review of the existing June 24 card and the April 27 liturgical commemoration authorized in permitted places. The Marians also list June 24, so the dates may represent different observances. [Holy See decree](https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/la/apost_letters/2004/documents/hf_jp-ii_apl_20040425_eusebia-palomino-yenes.html); [Marians](https://marian.org/articles/north-american-sanctity-st-maria-guadalupe-garcia-zavala).
